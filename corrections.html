Corrections and Updates

Accuracy is a priority, but mistakes can occur. When a material error is identified, the newspaper reviews the story and corrects it clearly while retaining an auditable record of the change.

What May Require Correction?

Errors involving names, numbers, dates, places, attribution or wording that could turn an unverified claim into an apparent fact.

How Updates Work

News can change as new evidence appears. Material updates are reflected in the story and its verification status.

Reporting an Error

Correction requests should identify the article, disputed passage and supporting information or source.
