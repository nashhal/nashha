Editorial Policy

Al-Thawra Newspaper starts with the event itself, gathers evidence, evaluates the available material and distinguishes facts, attributed claims, interpretation and uncertainty before publication.

Core Principles

  • Do not build a story by simply repeating another outlet's headline.
  • Prioritize primary sources and material closest to the event.
  • Use appropriate independent evidence for sensitive reporting.
  • Keep facts, claims, interpretation and possibility visibly distinct.
  • Lower verification status or withhold publication when evidence is insufficient.
  • Write accurate headlines without promotional or sensational language.

Corrections

Discovered errors are reviewed and corrected clearly. Material changes remain traceable.

Source Independence

The presence of a source in the verification process does not mean the newspaper adopts that source's position.
