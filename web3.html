Web3 Layer: Record Integrity Without Replacing the Newsroom

The goal is to give readers an independent way to verify that a specific manifest has not changed after anchoring. The content remains off-chain while the network stores a compact fingerprint proof.

What Goes on Web3?

On-chain

Manifest fingerprint, CID when IPFS is used, anchoring time and the anchoring account identity defined by the smart contract.

manifestHash → anchorManifest(manifestHash, cid)

Off-chain

Story text, images, site pages and detailed verification materials remain off-chain to preserve performance and reduce cost and storage.

article + media + evidence → Southern Revolution infrastructure
Important: A blockchain transaction proves that a fingerprint for a specific record was anchored and unchanged on that chain; it does not by itself prove that every sentence in a story is true.
