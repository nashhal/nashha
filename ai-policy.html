Artificial Intelligence Policy

AI is used as an assisting newsroom tool, not as a replacement for editorial judgment or verification.

What the System Does

  • Monitors and organizes newly discovered material.
  • Helps connect reports to the same event.
  • Assists with summaries, categorization and draft preparation.
  • Flags material that needs additional review.

What It Must Not Do

  • Turn an unverified claim into a reported fact.
  • Invent names, numbers, dates, quotes or links.
  • Be treated as independent evidence simply because output was generated by AI.

Accountability

Published material remains subject to the newspaper's editorial standards, source records, verification process and correction policy.
