Artificial Intelligence Policy

The Southern Revolution uses AI as a newsroom assistance layer for discovery, organization, analysis and draft preparation. AI output is not treated as independent evidence and does not replace source review or editorial verification.

Assistance only · Human editorial responsibility

What the AI Layer Does

  • Monitors and organizes newly discovered material.
  • Helps group reports that concern the same event.
  • Assists with summaries, categorization and editorial draft preparation.
  • Produces structured analysis fields when a published item is eligible for analysis.
  • Flags incomplete or uncertain material for additional review.

What the AI Layer Must Not Do

  • Convert an unverified claim into an established fact.
  • Invent names, dates, figures, quotations, sources or links.
  • Replace the original source record or evidence.
  • Use an AI-generated answer as proof simply because it sounds plausible.

Editorial Verification

Published reporting remains tied to its source record and verification status. Attributed claims are kept distinct from established facts, and uncertain material can remain under review rather than being presented as confirmed reporting.

Current Implementation

The automated newsroom is implemented in GitHub Actions. The analysis pipeline uses an xAI API model configured by the repository workflow; the exact runtime model is a deployment setting and should not be inferred from this policy page.

Important: AI assistance can improve organization and consistency, but it cannot establish the truth of a report on its own.
